# Showmyrise Advertising Agency

Advertising management for independent Amazon sellers.

Showmyrise Advertising Agency is an independent advertising agency run by Rodney Benito. It manages advertising on Amazon for sellers in the US, Canada, the UK, Europe and Australia.

## What I do
I manage Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display ads for Amazon sellers who want their advertising run carefully and reported clearly. For each seller I work with, I:
- Start with a free account audit. Before any work begins, I review the seller's advertising and show where spend is being wasted and what I would change. The seller chooses how: by authorizing the tool through Amazon, or by sending their own Amazon reports.
- Run their campaigns. I set up and structure Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display campaigns, adjust bids and budgets, add keywords, targets and negatives, and pause what isn't working.
- Report on performance. Weekly and monthly reports cover spend, sales, ACoS, TACoS and what changed, with recommendations for the next period.
- Connect ads to the bigger picture. I compare advertising results with total sales and product costs, so decisions are based on profit, not just ad metrics.

## How the tool works
Showmyrise Advertising Agency uses a private tool for this work. It is not offered to the public, and sellers don't need to install or log in to anything.
- A seller authorizes it once through Amazon's own sign-in page, and can revoke that access at any time.
- It uses the official Amazon Ads API to read campaign performance and to make the advertising changes listed above.
- It uses the Amazon Selling Partner API to read sales and traffic by product and fee totals, so reports can show total sales and profit.
- It doesn't change product listings, prices, inventory or orders, and it doesn't access Amazon customers' personal information.

Every change is made deliberately by me, as part of the work the seller has asked me to do.

## Privacy and data
Data is used only to manage and report on the authorizing seller's advertising. It is never sold. It is stored on encrypted devices and deleted within 30 days when the work ends or on request. Full details, including the services that help process it, are in the [Privacy Notice](https://showmyrise.com/privacy/).
